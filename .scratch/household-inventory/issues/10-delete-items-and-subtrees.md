# 10 — Members permanently delete Items and subtrees

**What to build:** A Member can permanently delete a childless Item or confirm removal of an Item subtree after reviewing exactly what will be lost.

**Blocked by:** 06 — Members add optional Item photos camera-first; 08 — Members find Items through Household search.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A Member can permanently delete a childless non-root Item after confirmation.
- [ ] Deleting an Item with Child Items shows the selected Item, its complete Item Path, and the exact descendant count.
- [ ] The subtree warning states that the selected Item, every descendant, and their photos will be permanently deleted.
- [ ] Subtree deletion remains disabled until the Member types the selected Item's displayed name exactly.
- [ ] Confirming subtree deletion removes the selected Item and every descendant.
- [ ] Deleting an Item or subtree also permanently removes every associated stored photo.
- [ ] Deleted Items disappear from browsing and search results.
- [ ] The Household root cannot be deleted through Item actions.
- [ ] No trash, undo, restore, or Parent Item history is offered.
- [ ] Automated checks cover childless deletion, descendant counting, typed-name confirmation, subtree removal, root protection, search removal, and photo cleanup.
