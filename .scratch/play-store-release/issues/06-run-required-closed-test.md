# 06: Run the required closed test with at least 12 Members

**What to build:** Recruit and support a stable closed-test cohort that satisfies Google Play's production-access requirement while producing useful evidence about real use, reliability, and the clarity of the app's core Household inventory journey.

**Blocked by:** 04

**Status:** ready-for-human

- [ ] At least 12 eligible testers opt in to the closed test through Google Play and remain continuously opted in for at least 14 days, with dates and cohort size recorded.
- [ ] The cohort covers a reasonable mix of supported Android versions and device types rather than twelve registrations on a single device configuration.
- [ ] Testers receive a concise test charter covering sign-in, Household Access, Item and Photo management, Search, Description Generation, Account Deletion, and how to report feedback.
- [ ] Feedback is collected in a structured form, triaged, and linked to local issues when it identifies defects or material usability problems.
- [ ] Crash, ANR, sign-in, App Check, and backend health signals are reviewed during the test, and release-blocking regressions are resolved before the production-access application.
- [ ] Evidence suitable for Google Play's production-access questionnaire summarizes participation, usage, feedback, fixes, and the final health of the tested release without exposing tester personal data.
