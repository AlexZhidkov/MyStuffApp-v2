# Delete an Owner's Household and Anonymize a Deleted Member's Content

When a non-Owner deletes their MyStuff Account, remove their membership and every bound or unclaimed Household Access for their verified Google email, but preserve shared Household content with non-identifying Former Member Attribution. Ordinary Owner-initiated access removal continues retaining real historical attribution. When a Household Owner deletes their MyStuff Account, delete the entire Household and its data rather than transfer ownership. A later sign-in with the same Google Account creates a new MyStuff Account without restoring former access or attribution. This preserves the established single-Owner model and protects content shared with a Household, accepting that an Owner's deletion removes the Inventory for every Member; it supersedes ADR-0007's assumption that Household Access ends only when the Household Owner removes it.
