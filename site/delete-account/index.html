If you cannot send from the original address, email azhidkov@gmail.com and explain the situation. Additional verification may be required.

What will be deleted

MyStuff removes the account, Firebase Authentication record, memberships, and matching Household Access. It does not delete your Google Account. For a non-Owner, shared Items and Photos remain in the Household but personal attribution is replaced with “Former member.” For a Household Owner, the entire Household and its shared data are permanently deleted for every Member.

Access is removed when deletion starts. Restricted provider recovery copies and security or abuse logs may remain temporarily under the retention periods described in the Privacy Policy.
