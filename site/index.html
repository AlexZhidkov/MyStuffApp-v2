MyStuff

Privacy Policy

Effective 11 September 2026

MyStuff is a shared Household Inventory app developed by Alex Zhidkov in Western Australia, Australia. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to azhidkov@gmail.com.

Information MyStuff handles

  • Your Google and Firebase identity, including an account identifier, name, and email address.
  • Household membership and access email addresses.
  • Item names, descriptions, Tags, web links, Photos, and creator or updater attribution.
  • Search queries and derived Search vectors.
  • Item Photos and text submitted when you request Description Generation.
  • Technical security data processed by Google services, such as IP address, device, app, integrity, and request information.

How the information is used

This information is used only to authenticate Members, protect Household access, store and synchronize the Inventory, upload and display Photos, provide Search and Description Generation, prevent abuse, and operate the service. MyStuff does not contain advertising or analytics, and Alex Zhidkov does not sell personal information.

Cloud processing and sharing

MyStuff uses Google services including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage for Firebase, Cloud Functions for Firebase, Firebase App Check with Play Integrity, Firebase AI Logic, and the paid Gemini Developer API. Google and its subprocessors process information to provide those services. Firebase Authentication data is processed in the United States; other service data may be processed where Google or its subprocessors operate.

Search sends Item names, Tags, descriptions, and Member queries to Gemini to create embeddings, which Cloud Firestore compares. MyStuff does not store raw Search queries, although providers may keep operational logs. Description Generation sends the selected Item Photo, Item name, existing description, and device language to Gemini when a Member requests it. Under the paid Gemini terms, prompts and responses are not used to improve Google products. Gemini may retain abuse-monitoring copies for up to 55 days.

This privacy site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor IP addresses and related request information for security and service operation.

Storage, security, and retention

Active Household information remains stored while the Household exists. Access is protected through Firebase Authentication, Firebase security rules, and App Check. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

When deletion starts, MyStuff immediately removes app access and deletes active data asynchronously. Restricted provider recovery copies and security or abuse logs may remain temporarily under provider retention schedules. Firebase Authentication backup copies may take up to 180 days to expire, and Gemini abuse-monitoring copies may remain for up to 55 days. MyStuff does not intentionally keep a separate account archive.

Account and Household deletion

In the Android app, open the menu and select Delete account. You must confirm your Google Account before deletion begins. If you are a non-Owner, your MyStuff account, Firebase Authentication record, personal attribution, membership, and matching Household Access are removed; your Google Account itself is not deleted; shared Items and Photos remain with the non-identifying attribution “Former member.” If you own a Household, deleting your account also permanently deletes that Household, its memberships, Items, Search data, attachments, and Photos for every Member.

A Household Owner may instead select Delete Household. This deletes the shared Household data while keeping the Owner's MyStuff Account. A later sign-in after Account Deletion creates a new account and does not restore previous access or attribution.

If you cannot use the app, the public account-deletion page lets you request deletion by email.

Your choices

Household Owners control Household Access. Members can avoid Description Generation by not requesting it, but cloud processing remains part of Search and the other online app features. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information where applicable law provides that right by contacting azhidkov@gmail.com.

Children

MyStuff is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required by local law. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided personal information should contact azhidkov@gmail.com.
